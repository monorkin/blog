Last July I started working at [37signals](https://37signals.com/), the company
behind Basecamp, Hey & Fizzy and the place from which the very first version or
Ruby On Rails was extracted back in 2004.

It's still the company with the largest influence on the direction of Rails
development with major contributions like ActiveStorage, SolidQueue, Turbo &
Stimulus.

I'll talk about how the company works and what it's like to work there.
